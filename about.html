Who We Are

Pondicherry's Premier Bicycle Destination

Founded over 15 years ago in the heart of Pondicherry, Cycle World began with a simple belief — that the right bicycle can transform how you experience the world around you.

What started as a small neighbourhood showroom has grown into the city's most trusted cycling destination, offering an unmatched selection of premium bicycles from the finest brands across India and beyond.

From mountain trails to city commutes, children's first rides to serious road cycling — we've guided over 3,000 customers in finding their perfect ride. Our team brings genuine passion and deep expertise to every conversation.
